About

I'm a wireless engineer at Huawei in Ottawa, where I work on Wi-Fi sensing: using the signals that wireless systems already exchange to sense what is happening in the space around them.

Most of my time goes into the signal processing and machine-learning models that turn raw wireless measurements into reliable information, and into making sure those algorithms hold up as part of a real system rather than only in simulation.

I did my PhD in electrical engineering at the University of Alberta, finishing with a 4/4 GPA. My research focused on the physical layer of wireless communication, including 6G systems, reconfigurable intelligent surfaces, integrated sensing and communication, ambient backscatter, and deep learning for channel estimation and signal detection. What I enjoy most is taking an idea that starts as math and getting it to work reliably on real hardware.

Research interests

01

Wi-Fi Sensing

CSI processing for activity recognition, presence detection, and through-the-air perception in IEEE 802.11 systems.

02

mmWave Radar

Radar-based perception and gesture recognition, with feature extraction tuned for low false-alarm sensing.

03

Integrated Sensing & Comm.

ISAC beamforming, multi-target detection, and CRB minimization for joint sensing and communication.

04

AI / ML for Wireless

A broad range of machine-learning and deep-learning methods applied to wireless problems, from channel estimation and signal detection to reliable classification under real-world conditions.

05

Optimization & Beamforming

Convex optimization, SDR, manifold methods, and alternating optimization for resource allocation.

06

PHY / MAC Layer Design

Physical- and MAC-layer algorithm and protocol design for 5G, 6G, and Wi-Fi systems.
